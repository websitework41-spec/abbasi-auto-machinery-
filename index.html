How to Buy From Us

Our vehicle purchasing process is simple, transparent, and hassle-free.

Settled in USD by 100% advance T/T to ABBASI TRADING LLC · Gunma Bank.

  1. 1

    Select Your Vehicle

    Choose your preferred vehicle(s) from our available stock, or send us a list of vehicles you regularly purchase.

  2. 2

    Finalize Your Deal

    Once you have selected your desired vehicle(s), we will finalize the deal and confirm all purchase details with you.

  3. 3

    Provide Consignee Details

    Share your complete consignee and shipping information with us so we can proceed with the necessary documentation.

  4. 4

    Receive Your Proforma Invoice

    We will reserve the vehicle(s) under your name and issue a Proforma Invoice (PI) containing all relevant payment and transaction details.

  5. 5

    Make Payment

    Make the payment through your bank using the banking details provided in the Proforma Invoice.

  6. 6

    Vehicle Shipment

    Once your payment has been confirmed, we will arrange shipment of your vehicle(s) from Japan to your designated destination.

  7. 7

    Receive Original Documents

    Once the vehicle(s) have departed from Japan, we will dispatch the original vehicle documents to your registered postal address via DHL.

Banking & Trade Terms

Official Remittance & Export Policy

Direct settlement in USD to Gunma Bank, Japan. Transparent trade terms and compliant export procedures under FOB, CIF, or C&F.

Official Bank Remittance

Gunma Bank · Japan

Fixed Bank Surcharge: A standard fee of USD 25 must be remitted over the invoice total to cover receiving bank charges in Japan.

Anti-Fraud Advisory: Please verify all vehicle chassis numbers and remittance instructions before proceeding with payment.

Standard Export Terms

Trade Protocol
  • 1
    100% Advance T/T: Full invoice settlement is required in USD ($) via Telegraphic Transfer directly to our designated Gunma Bank account prior to vessel loading.
  • 2
    Trade IncoTerms: Quotes provided under FOB (Yokohama/Kobe), CIF, or C&F as agreed. Shipping arranged on scheduled RORO or container sailings.
  • 3
    Export Documentation: Original Bill of Lading (B/L) and Japan Export Certificate are promptly couriered via DHL once the vessel departs Japan.
  • 4
    Stock Allocation & Priority: Vehicles and machinery are subject to prior sale. Bookings are officially confirmed solely upon cleared receipt of funds.
  • 5
    Inspected Condition ("AS-IS"): Units are sold strictly "AS-IS, WHERE-IS" with verified Japanese auction inspection grades and chassis details.

Irrevocable Acceptance: Remitting payment, confirming a Proforma Invoice (PI), or acting on an order constitutes full acceptance of all ABBASI TRADING LLC commercial export terms.
